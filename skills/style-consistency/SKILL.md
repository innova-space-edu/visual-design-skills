# Style Consistency

Create a style fingerprint from the approved reference:
palette, contrast, line quality, texture, material response, lighting direction, camera grammar, typography roles, border radius, icon treatment, spacing, and background behavior.

Reuse the fingerprint across the series.
Do not force identical composition; preserve visual system, not duplicated layouts.
